How the wait is calculated

    Each scenario reruns the whole calculation, from arrival rate to wait. The scenarios start from the settings on Explore; at the data as given, the current state is the validated hand calculation. Cost of the wait is holding cost only (Lq × cost per lot-hour × 8,760 h a year); see the Costs page for the assumptions.

    The cost of waiting here is holding cost only. Waiting also delays revenue and can lower yield, and at a bottleneck the bigger cost is lost output, which this figure doesn’t include.
